## Wounds & Death
- Wounds count **up**, not down. `x` is how many you've taken; `y` is your own personal Down threshold — different per Character.
- **Down** ≠ out. A Down Character can still be targeted, and can't act unaided, but isn't removed from play.
- **Death Wounds** is a separate, species-fixed ceiling (Humanoid: 10) — crossing it is what actually kills you, not reaching your own `y`.
- **Execution**: attacking an already-Down target skips Block and Flow entirely — guaranteed +1 Wound, -1 Life.
- **Obliteration** is different again — every Body Part gone. Rare, not the same as Death Wounds.
- **Life** reaching 0 doesn't erase you — it means "no longer self-sustaining," not "no body."

## Core Resources
- **Flow** is your personal shield — Recovery refills it each round.
- **Focus / Lightning / Lava** are Technique fuel — Attunement refills all three, each round, in full (not split between them).
- **Resilience** shrinks every incoming hit and is what lets you take damage past your own `y`, toward the universal ceiling.
- **Control** sets your AP cost per action — higher Control, cheaper actions. Control 0 means you can't act without help.

## Techniques
- Four categories: **Flow Powers, Focus Powers, Lightning Crafts, Lava Crafts.**
- A Character needs a category switched on to use anything in it — checked on the Character Sheet.
- Techniques used on an Ally that *help* (Embolden, Anchor) are never treated as an attack.

## Party & Possession
- Your **Mini-Party** rides on your own Tile — up to 4 members.
- A **Mob** is any group of Characters sharing one Tile — get crowded enough (3+) and it starts affecting movement and line of sight.
- Gom and Kulkrow ambiently possess *everyone* by default (Shadow Possession) — that's not a special event, it's the baseline every Character already has in their Blood.
- **Veil Vision** toggles between "what your Character would actually know" and the full picture — Enemy intent stays hidden until it resolves unless it's on.
