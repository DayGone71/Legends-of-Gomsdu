## The same dark joke, one scale up (seeded 2026-08-22)

**Mud People** have Hearts, but no Glee for Head. **Crystal Beings** have Hearts, but no Anguish for Head — the same exclusive-to-specific-Characters pattern the First War's own three Axes use (see `scenes.md`'s First Era Legend), just no longer restricted to the two brothers alone.

**The two Hearts aren't the same organ, per dDd**: a Crystal Being's is a Rainbow Sliver (a physical fragment of a Rainbow Shard — see `gomsdu-codex.md`'s Blood/Factions sections); a Mud Person's is "a bundle in the center of the Mud, like a Root Cluster" — different Biology, functionally equivalent, and not recognized as the same thing by either culture. Crystal Beings build **Spires** out of ZON (a Crystal Structure — see `gomsdu-codex.md`'s "Language as the Animating Gate"). Mud People dig **Mud Pits** into the Planet — the same downward-vs-upward, inward-vs-outward contrast running through the rest of the cosmology.

**dDd's own read on what this actually is**: "The Second Era takes Psychological Themes and makes them Sociological, with the same dark joke angle applied to a new scale." The First Era's dark joke is one Character's survival-through-disengagement (Stand Around). Whatever the Second Era's own version turns out to be, it's a *society's* version of the same move — Spires reaching up and Pits reaching down read like two civilizations each finding their own large-scale way to not look directly at something. Not asserted as settled — a resonance worth noticing, not a design decision made on dDd's behalf.
